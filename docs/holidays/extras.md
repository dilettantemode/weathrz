2027 fast starts on 8 Feb, its official Awal Ramadan, a day before Pakistan's and Bangladesh's; the 2028 dates
are estimates too, with Ramadan 1449 starting 28 Jan in Malaysia and about 29 Jan in Pakistan
and Bangladesh, and Eid about 27 Feb in all three). The greeting
shows for the first three days, as Indonesia's does; the crescent hangs all month.
